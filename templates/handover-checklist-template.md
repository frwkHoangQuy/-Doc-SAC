- The repository is public; record only sanitized status and references, never credentials or sensitive handover content.

## Status vocabulary

- **Not Started:** Verification has not begun.
- **Requested:** Evidence or action has been requested.
- **Received:** Evidence or an item has been received but not yet verified.
- **In Review:** Supplied evidence is being evaluated.
- **Gap:** Required evidence or capability is missing or insufficient.
- **Not Applicable:** Human authority has confirmed the item does not apply.
- **Verified:** Evidence has been reviewed and accepted by the designated authority.

`Verified` must not be used without cited evidence and explicit reviewer acceptance. This blank template initializes every category as `Not Started`.

## Evidence handling

- Store only public-safe, sanitized evidence in this repository.
- Link to access-controlled evidence when authorized.

- A requested or supplied item is not automatically verified.
- Record discovered risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies through the RAID review process.
