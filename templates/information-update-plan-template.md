## Validation, safety, and failure boundaries

Specify staged ordinal equality, modes/operations, metadata/IDs, links, Markdown, full-diff inspection, `git diff --check`, credential/value and restricted-identity scans, ancestry/counts, live remote/Issue/PR read-back, and clean state. Define rollback/partial-state handling at every commit and push boundary without amend, rebase, reset, force, deletion, compensation, or guessed state.

## Exclusions and exact stop
